What it does

Everything between “I found a role” and “resume sent”.

Rule-based matching you can audit, AI rewrites you can reject, and exports that are real files.

Transparent match score

50% required skills, 20% preferred skills, 20% projects & experience, 10% education. Not-applicable categories are redistributed and explained.

Truthful AI tailoring

Rewrites only rephrase existing facts. New numbers and unsupported skills are rejected by a validator before they ever reach your resume.

Live editor, three themes

Two-panel editing, instant preview, drag-free reordering, visibility toggles, undo/redo and autosave to SQLite.

Real PDF & DOCX

A4 pages, consistent margins, wrapped text, no clipped content — and a Word document with editable paragraphs and list bullets.

Safety by default

API keys stay on the server, URLs are SSRF-checked, inputs validated, all SQL parameterised and every suggestion requires your confirmation.

Honest by design

No random scores, no invented internships, no promises about ATS. If the AI call fails the app says so and falls back to deterministic rules.

The rule we don’t break

Your resume stays yours.

ResumeIQ never adds a degree, employer, skill or metric that you did not enter. Missing skills are shown as gaps to learn — not as things to fake.
