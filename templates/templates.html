What “export-ready” means here

No text is placed inside images or absolute-positioned boxes, so parsers can read it.

PDF
  • A4 page, 17 mm side margins, automatic page breaks
  • Long words wrap instead of clipping
  • Sections are kept together — no orphaned headings
DOCX
  • Editable paragraphs and real paragraph headings
  • Native Word list bullets
  • Same section order and visibility as the draft
Honesty
  • Exports use the latest autosaved content only
  • Switching template never changes your text
  • No ATS score is promised anywhere
